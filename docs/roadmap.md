## Customer CRM v0.8.0

- Persisted customer accounts and lifecycle status.
- Searchable CRM dashboard with commercial KPIs.
- Create, profile, edit, archive and restore workflows.
- Contact, billing, address, credit and payment-term management.

## Finance Centre v0.9.0

- Persisted customer invoices with calculated VAT and balances.
- Billing KPIs for invoiced, collected, outstanding and overdue value.
- Search and filter by customer, status, invoice number and reference.
- Create, profile and edit workflows with payment tracking.

## Smart Contract Marketplace v1.1.0

- Persisted freight opportunities linked to active customer accounts.
- Draft and published contract states with routes, cargo, dates, budget and governed terms.
- Competitive carrier bidding with commercial proposals and delivery estimates.
- Atomic award workflow that accepts the winning bid and rejects the remaining proposals.
- Marketplace KPIs, search, status filters and role-based access.
